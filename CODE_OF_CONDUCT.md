# Code of Conduct

TaskForge should be a useful, respectful place to collaborate.

## Expected Behavior

- Be kind and professional.
- Assume good intent, but accept correction when something lands badly.
- Keep criticism focused on ideas, code, docs, and operational tradeoffs.
- Make room for newcomers and for people with different levels of context.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Demeaning language or repeated dismissiveness.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Sustained off-topic disruption in issues, reviews, or discussions.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate, edit, or remove comments and may restrict participation for behavior that harms the project.
If you experience or observe unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately with relevant context.
